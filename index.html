About Back to Roots

Who we Are?

We create research-informed, nature-driven learning experiences that strengthen the foundation of childhood. Our programs are intentionally designed to build emotional resilience, creativity, sensory awareness, and social confidence through meaningful hands-on exploration. By blending nature, culture, and experiential learning, we offer children high-quality developmental experiences that enrich classroom learning.

Our Mission

At Back to Roots, we strive to rekindle the essence of childhood by creating meaningful, hands-on experiences that nurture a child's curiosity, imagination, and connection with nature and culture. Through thoughtful play and creative exploration, we inspire a slower, more mindful way of growing and learning — filled with real moments, simple joy, and lasting memories.

Seeds of Wonder — Stories Rooted in Childhood

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam — The Dreamer Who Flew Kites and Ideas

Growing up in Rameswaram, young Kalam loved watching birds and flying kites. He tinkered with toy boats and model planes, inspired by the sea and sky. That playful curiosity later defined his career as India's "Missile Man" and visionary President.

Root → Growth: Childhood fascination with flight → Lifelong pursuit of innovation and inspiration.
Reflection: Does our kid have any hobby of his own other than the ones doing in extra classes under supervision?

Walt Disney — The Boy Who Drew Mice and Dreams

Walt Disney grew up on a farm in Missouri, surrounded by animals and nature. As a child, he would spend hours sketching the creatures around him and entertaining his siblings with homemade drawings. His love for storytelling and fascination with animals planted the seed of thought for why not the characters in drawing move which resulted in the idea of Animation.

Root → Growth: Childhood sketches of animals → Birth of animation's most beloved world.
Reflection: Does our kid have space and time in a day to do something out of regular routine?

Rabindranath Tagore — The Poet of Nature's Classroom

As a child in Bengal, Tagore spent hours gazing at trees, birds, and the changing sky. His family's garden became his first classroom — a place where imagination met observation. The rhythm of nature later echoed in his poems and the school he founded, Shantiniketan, where children learned under open skies.

Root → Growth: Wonder for nature → Vision for holistic, creative education.
Reflection: Are we letting our kid free time to observe the sky, a leaf, or a bark in nature?

R.K. Laxman — The Boy Who Doodled the Everyday

In Mysore, a little boy drew on walls, doors, and old notebooks — sketching faces he saw around town. His childhood doodles became his lifelong language. Laxman's sharp eye for human quirks led to the creation of India's most beloved observer — The Common Man.

Root → Growth: Playful doodling → Timeless storytelling through cartoons.
Reflection: Are we judging everytime our kid draws something imaginary?

Steven Spielberg — The Boy Who Made Movies in His Backyard

As a young boy in Arizona, Steven Spielberg was always curious about how stories could come alive. Using his father's 8mm camera, he filmed toy trains crashing, friends acting in adventure scenes & clay models. His backyard became his first film studio, where play met purpose. Those childhood experiments shaped him into one of the world's greatest storytellers — bringing wonder, fear, and imagination to screens everywhere.

Root → Growth: Backyard movie-making → Cinematic magic that reshaped storytelling.
Reflection: Is your child playing in their own imaginative way or stuck in same child playing area - slides /swings every evening?

🌿 Every great story begins with a seed — a moment of play, a connection with nature, a spark of curiosity. Back to Roots celebrates those beginnings, reminding us that the truest growth happens when we nurture what once made us wonder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I get to see photos/videos of my child’s session?
We capture sweet moments during each workshop and share curated photos and videos with parents in a link afterwards. It’s a great way to witness their joy and growth.
Why doesnt B2R have a dedicated centre?
Back to Roots is not a place, its a way of learning, living and growing. B2R mission is to reconnect children with real world settings and not confine them into a building. We have witnessed real learning happens in open spaces and community environments.
Is the session supervised at all times?
Absolutely. Safety is our priority. All sessions are facilitated by trained adults who maintain a warm, watchful environment. We work with small groups to ensure every child is seen, heard, and cared for.
How are your sessions different from regular extracurricular classes?
We focus on experience, not instruction. Kids don’t just sit and practice, they craft, plant, move, laugh, and reflect. It’s unplugged, grounded in Indian culture, and full of creative freedom helping build emotional intelligence, observation skills, and joy.
Do you collaborate with schools or communities for private batches?
Yes! We love working with schools, apartments, and learning communities. If you are an educator or parent who wants to bring Back to Roots to your campus or group, just reach out — we’ll customize it for you, while alligning with our Phyilosophy.
